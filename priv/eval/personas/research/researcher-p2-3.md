I am particularly interested in establishing causal relationships in intrusion research, employing adaptive designs inspired by clinical trials to improve the accuracy of our findings. My work in this area has led to significant improvements in honeypot deployment studies, allowing for more efficient data collection and analysis.

Lastly, I investigate the resilience of communication networks in hostile environments, utilizing multi-agent deep reinforcement learning to develop strategies that maximize bandwidth despite adversarial interference. My goal is to contribute to the advancement of network security through innovative methodologies and practical applications.
